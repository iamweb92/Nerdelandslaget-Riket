# Nerdelandslaget: Riket

**One guild chat, one member list and one professions board for every guild with "nerd" in its name, right in the guild window (J).** Made by Neophyte for Nerdelandslaget, Nerdelandslaget II and their friends on WoW: Forever.

> Riket only does something for players in a guild whose name contains "nerd". In any other guild it stays quiet.

## What it does

- **One guild chat:** the guild chat in the guild window (J) shows the /g of every nerd guild together, in time order, as `[Nerdelandslaget II] [Name]: text`. You write in the window's own box as always. Officer chat is never mixed.
- **Members:** a tab with who is online in all the nerd guilds, with level, class, guild, guild rank and Discord name. Click a column to sort, click it again to turn the order round. Click a name to whisper, right-click to invite. Tick "Show offline players" to also see who was online in the last 30 days.
- **Professions:** a tab with everyone's professions, skill and a link to their recipes. Shift-click a link to put it in chat.
- **Discord:** a tab where every player sets their Discord name. It is required: Riket stays off until it is set. Where your rank may edit guild notes, one click puts it in your guild note too.

## Getting started

1. Install Riket and log in.
2. Press **J**, open the **Discord** tab on the window's right side and write your Discord name.
3. Open each of your profession windows once, so Riket can share their links.

Only players with Riket see the other guilds' chat and lists. One player with Riket online in each guild is enough.

## Commands

`/riket` (or `/nll`) opens Riket in the guild window. Also: `/riket members`, `/riket professions`, `/riket discord`, `/riket check` (what Riket sees in the guild window), `/riket status` and `/riket button` (shows or hides the minimap button).

## Good to know

- During boss encounters the game hides guild chat from addons, so those lines are not passed on.
- Riket uses its own hidden channel (`NLRiketNet`) and its own saved data, and runs alongside other Nerdelandslaget addons.
